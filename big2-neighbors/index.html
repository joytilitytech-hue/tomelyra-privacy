Big 2 Neighbors — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Joytility Tech develops Big 2 Neighbors, an Android card game. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Offline gameplay and information on your device

You do not need to create an account with Joytility Tech. Gameplay runs offline against computer-controlled opponents. The app stores your saved game, selected character, scores, story points, unlocked chapters, game statistics, daily free-game allowance, and sound preferences in its private storage on your device. This information is used to resume games, preserve progress, apply the daily allowance, and remember settings. The app does not upload this gameplay information to Joytility Tech.

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location, and does not use an advertising identifier.

Google Play purchases

If you choose to unlock the Full edition, payment is handled by Google Play. The app receives purchase information, including the product identifier, purchase status, purchase token, and signed receipt. It uses this information to verify and acknowledge your purchase, enable Full access, and restore ownership through Google Play. A purchase receipt and entitlement information are stored locally. Joytility Tech does not receive your payment-card details, and the app does not send purchase receipts to a Joytility Tech server.

Google processes purchase and service information under its own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy. Buying or restoring Full access requires access to Google Play and may require an internet connection.

Support requests

If you contact Joytility Tech by email, we receive your email address, your message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond and troubleshoot your request. Please avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information. Support correspondence is retained only for as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion using the contact below.

Sharing and sale of information

Joytility Tech does not sell your personal information or share local gameplay information for advertising. Google Play handles purchases as described above. Email providers process support correspondence. Information may be disclosed when legally required.

Retention, deletion, and security

Local app data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. To delete it, open Android Settings, select Apps, then Big 2 Neighbors, then Storage (or Storage & cache), and choose Clear storage (or Clear data). Menu names vary by device. This permanently removes saved games, scores, story progress, preferences, and locally saved purchase information. The app disables Android cloud backup and app-data transfer backups.

Deleting local data does not delete your Google Play transaction history or refund a purchase. A qualifying Full purchase can be restored using the same Google Play account. Google determines retention of its transaction records. Joytility Tech cannot restore deleted local game progress.

Local records are stored in Android's app-private storage. Purchase receipts are verified before Full access is granted. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children's privacy

The app does not ask children to provide personal information and does not provide chat or public profiles. A parent or guardian should supervise purchases. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us in a support request, contact us so we can address it.

This policy webpage

This page is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as an IP address, to operate and secure the hosting service. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. This page contains no analytics scripts or advertising.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or our data practices change. The latest version will appear on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or support-data deletion requests, contact Joytility Tech at joytilitytech+big2support@gmail.com. Include “Big 2 Neighbors” in the subject so we can identify the app.
