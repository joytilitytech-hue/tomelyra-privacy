TomeLyra Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the TomeLyra Android application ("TomeLyra", "the app", "we", or "us") handles information when you use the app.

TomeLyra is designed as a local-first reading and text-to-speech application. Its primary purpose is to allow users to open supported documents and ebooks and listen to or read their content on their own device.

1. Information TomeLyra Accesses

TomeLyra may access files that you explicitly select, including supported PDF, EPUB, and MOBI documents.

The app may process information contained in those files, including:

  • Book or document text
  • Title and author information when available
  • Reading position and playback progress
  • Bookmarks and library organization information
  • The device reference or URI needed to reopen a selected document

This information is used to provide TomeLyra's reading, narration, library, bookmark, and playback features.

2. Local Processing

Book and document content selected in TomeLyra is processed locally on your device for the app's normal reading and narration functions. TomeLyra does not upload your book or document contents to servers operated by the TomeLyra developer.

TomeLyra stores certain app information locally on your device, such as library entries, reading progress, bookmarks, settings, downloaded text-to-speech resources, and generated narration cache files.

3. Text-to-Speech

TomeLyra can use locally installed or downloaded text-to-speech technology.

For supported offline voices, TomeLyra may download voice or model files from third-party hosting services such as GitHub or associated content delivery networks. After those resources are downloaded, supported offline narration processing occurs on your device.

When downloading these resources, the hosting provider may receive ordinary network information associated with the request, such as your IP address, device networking information, and request time. Such information is handled according to the privacy practices of the third-party hosting provider.

TomeLyra may also allow you to use the Android System Text-to-Speech engine installed or selected on your device. System text-to-speech engines are provided by Android, your device manufacturer, or another third party and may operate according to their own privacy policies and data practices. TomeLyra does not control those third-party engines.

4. Internet Access

TomeLyra may use an Internet connection to download optional text-to-speech models or voice resources and for functionality that requires access to those externally hosted resources.

The app does not require a TomeLyra user account to read supported local documents.

5. Analytics and Advertising

TomeLyra does not include advertising and does not use developer-operated analytics to track your reading activity.

TomeLyra does not sell your personal information.

6. Sensitive Device Permissions

TomeLyra does not require access to your microphone, contacts, precise location, camera, or call history for its normal reading and narration features.

Documents are selected using Android's document-selection mechanisms, which allow you to choose the files that TomeLyra may access.

7. Generated Narration and Offline Content

TomeLyra may generate and temporarily store narration audio in the app's private storage so that narration can be played efficiently or made available for offline listening within TomeLyra.

TomeLyra does not provide a feature for exporting an entire book as an MP3 file.

Cached narration can be removed through TomeLyra's available storage or offline-content controls or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

8. Android Backup

TomeLyra is configured to exclude its private application data from Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup mechanisms where supported by Android. This includes locally stored preferences, database information, downloaded models, and app-private narration files.

9. Data Sharing

TomeLyra does not transmit your selected book or document contents to servers operated by the TomeLyra developer.

Limited information may nevertheless be processed by third parties when you voluntarily use functionality supplied by those parties. Examples include:

  • Network requests made to download text-to-speech models
  • Use of a third-party or device-provided Android System TTS engine

Those services are governed by their respective terms and privacy policies.

10. Data Retention

Information maintained by TomeLyra is generally stored locally on your device for as long as it is needed to provide app functionality.

You can remove books from the TomeLyra library, remove cached narration where the app provides that option, clear TomeLyra's application data through Android settings, or uninstall TomeLyra.

Removing a book from TomeLyra's library does not necessarily delete the original document from the location where you stored it.

11. Data Security

TomeLyra is designed to minimize collection of user information and to keep document processing on the user's device whenever practical. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect app-local information. However, no software or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

12. Children's Privacy

TomeLyra does not knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-operated accounts, analytics, or advertising systems.

Parents or guardians who believe that information has been provided to the TomeLyra developer inappropriately may contact us using the address below.

13. Your Choices

You control which documents you select for use with TomeLyra. You may stop using the app, remove locally stored app information, clear application data through Android settings, or uninstall the app at any time.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when TomeLyra's functionality or data practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will indicate when the policy was most recently revised.
